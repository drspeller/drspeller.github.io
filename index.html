Profile

Profile

I work at the intersection of communication, education and the cultures of science and engineering.

At Lodz University of Technology, I design and teach courses for science and engineering students, with a particular interest in science communication, academic and technical communication, digital learning, and the role of the humanities in engineering education. I hold a PhD in Sociology from the University of Warwick.

The Engineering Imagination

A developing European initiative exploring how literature, history, philosophy, art and technological narratives can contribute to engineering education and help students think more imaginatively about technological choices, alternatives and consequences.

Work examining digital transformation in engineering education as a communicative problem: how disciplinary knowledge is selected, represented, visualised and made accessible across digital and multimodal environments.
